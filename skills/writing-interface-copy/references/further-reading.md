# Further reading: interface copy

Supplementary to [writing-interface-copy](../SKILL.md); nothing here is a rule the skill depends on. Each link below was opened in November 2026. For the books, title, author, publisher, year and ISBN come from the Open Library record cited; the publisher pages are linked for reference and may refuse automated requests.

## Style guides

- **GOV.UK, [Writing for user interfaces](https://www.gov.uk/service-manual/design/writing-for-user-interfaces)** (Service Manual): the shortest complete statement of cutting interface words, with before/after pairs.
- **Microsoft Writing Style Guide, [Use simple words, concise sentences](https://learn.microsoft.com/en-us/style-guide/word-choice/use-simple-words-concise-sentences)**: word-for-word swaps ("use", not "utilize"; "to", not "in order to") and the adverbs to cut.
- **Apple Human Interface Guidelines, [Writing](https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/writing)**: button labels, empty states, settings and error text on Apple platforms.
- **Atlassian Design System, [Designing messages](https://atlassian.design/foundations/content/designing-messages)**: error, warning, success, info and feature-discovery messages written out as patterns.

## Articles (Nielsen Norman Group)

- **Neusesser & Sunwall, [Error-Message Guidelines](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/error-message-guidelines/)** (2023): visibility, wording and efficiency guidelines for errors, with examples.
- **Loranger & Nielsen, [Microcontent: A Few Small Words Have a Mega Impact on Business](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/microcontent-how-to-write-headlines-page-titles-and-subject-lines/)** (2017): headings, titles and link text that stand alone.
- **Sherwin, ["Learn More" Links: You Can Do Better](https://www.nngroup.com/articles/learn-more-links/)** (2015): replacing vague link labels with descriptive ones.

## Books

- **Michael J. Metts & Andy Welfle, *Writing Is Designing: Words and the User Experience*** (Rosenfeld Media, 2020; ISBN 9781933820668; [Open Library OL33055592M](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL33055592M); [publisher](https://rosenfeldmedia.com/books/writing-is-designing/)): interface writing as design work, including testing words with users.
- **Torrey Podmajersky, *Strategic Writing for UX: Drive Engagement, Conversion, and Retention with Every Word*** (O'Reilly Media, 2019; ISBN 9781492049395; [Open Library OL28907920M](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL28907920M); [publisher](https://www.oreilly.com/library/view/strategic-writing-for/9781492049388/), which lists ISBN 9781492049388): voice charts and reusable patterns for UI text types.
- **Kinneret Yifrah, *Microcopy: The Complete Guide*** (Nemala, 2017; ISBN 9789655727944; [Open Library OL59227554M](https://openlibrary.org/books/OL59227554M); [author's site](https://www.microcopybook.com/)): microcopy by component, such as forms, errors, buttons and empty states.
